Modules & Main Features

Buildings, Rooms & Bed Allocation
  • Multi‑level structure: Buildings → Units → Rooms → Beds with capacity, room types and amenities.
  • Occupancy tracking: Real‑time occupancy status, vacant spaces, and occupancy rate KPIs.
  • Allocation tools: Quick views to find free beds, manage upgrades and move students.
Student Contracts & Rent Invoices
  • Contract lifecycle: Create contracts with start/end dates, room/bed, rent and security deposit.
  • Deposit handling: Track held vs refunded deposits and upgrades/downgrades.
  • Rent invoices: Monthly rent invoice model with due dates, paid/invoiced/overdue states and revenue analytics.
Attendance & Visitor Management
  • Daily attendance: Mark present/absent with date‑range reports and 7‑day charts.
  • Visitor register: Track visitor details, in/out time, relation and purpose with today & range‑based stats.
  • Security focus: See who is inside the hostel, pending visitors and daily history.
Maintenance, Complaints, Laundry & Inspections
  • Maintenance requests: Track issues by room/bed, priority and state (open, in progress, completed, cancelled).
  • Complaints module: Student complaint model with states and KPI widget for open complaints.
  • Laundry tracking: Laundry request model with in‑progress and completed states and counts on the dashboard.
  • Room inspections: Structured inspection forms & lines with scheduled and completed inspections, linked to rooms/beds.
Notice Board & Smart Dashboard
  • Announcements: Notice board module with categories, targets, effective dates and automatic expiry (cron).
  • Dashboard KPIs: Hero cards for occupancy, active students, vacant beds, revenue paid & outstanding.
  • Charts with filters: Occupancy by building, revenue (last 6 months), attendance (7d) and visitors (7d) – all driven by From/To date filters with clear calendar icons.

FAQs

1. Who is this Hostel Management System for?

This module is designed for schools, colleges, universities, training institutes and private hostel/PG operators who want to manage hostel operations directly inside Odoo 17 with a structured, scalable and report‑friendly system.

2. Does the dashboard support From/To date filters?

Yes. The hostel dashboard includes a From/To date range that affects statistics and charts such as check‑ins, contracts ending, attendance, visitors, maintenance, revenue, complaints and more. Widgets that are driven by the date filter are clearly marked with a calendar icon.

3. Can I track upcoming contract expiries?

The dashboard highlights contracts ending today, within 10 days and within 30 days, and provides quick‑action buttons to open the corresponding contract views for follow up, renewals or check‑out planning.

4. Is the module extendable for future needs?

Yes. The system is built using standard Odoo models, views and security rules, making it easy for developers to extend with additional reports, mobile apps, advanced security, email/SMS integration and custom workflows.
